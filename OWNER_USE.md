# Owner Use

This project is designed and supported for owner-installed software on a TV you
own, for privacy, transparency and local control. Do not install or test it on
someone else's TV. Network access or possession of credentials is not proof of
ownership. Respect household members' privacy and third-party accounts,
subscriptions and content rights. Unauthorized access, surveillance, credential
theft and service abuse are outside the project's purpose.

The underlying webOS firmware and services still run. Modules do not imply LG
endorsement, remove every applicable agreement, or guarantee that modifications
are permitted everywhere. They do not submit LG consent or impersonate an LG
account. Decline optional services and data-use permissions you do not want, or
review available consent-withdrawal controls and their consequences. Declining
consent is not a blanket exemption.

Read the module's actual behavior and restoration limits before enabling it.
Keep backups and independent root access. Separate repository boundaries and
warning text are organizational measures, not guarantees against takedowns,
liability or misuse. Publication is not a guarantee of compatibility or safety.
These are project and support expectations, not additional terms restricting
the MIT license.
